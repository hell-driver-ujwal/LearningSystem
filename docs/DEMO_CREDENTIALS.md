## eSewa demo payment

Paid courses (JavaScript Basics for the Browser NPR 499, Personal Finance for Students NPR 299, Statistics You Can Use NPR 399) can only be studied after a verified payment. Learners without one see the course page and its free preview lessons only.

1. Sign in as a learner who has not bought the course (for example Ben Lee) and open the course page.
2. Choose **Buy with eSewa**, then **Pay with eSewa** on the checkout page.
3. **eSewa ID:** any 10-digit mobile number starting with **97** or **98**, for example 9841234567.
4. **Verification code:** the **last 4 digits of that mobile number** (for 9841234567 the code is 4567).
5. The receipt appears and the learner is enrolled straight away.

Three wrong codes stop the payment (status Failed) and nothing is charged. Choosing Cancel marks the payment Canceled. No real money or real eSewa account is involved; everything runs on the local machine.
